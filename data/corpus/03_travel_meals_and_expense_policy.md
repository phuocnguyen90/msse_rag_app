# Corporate Travel, Meals, and Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-EXP-2025  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Applicability:** All Employees and Contractors incurring business-related expenses on behalf of Apex Technologies, Inc.

---

## 1. Overview and Core Principles
Employees conducting authorized business travel or incurring necessary operational expenses are entitled to prompt reimbursement. Apex Technologies expects staff to exercise sound fiscal judgment and prudent stewardship of company capital.

---

## 2. Air Travel and Transportation
- **Booking Channel:** All business flights must be booked through the corporate travel portal (Navan/Concur) at least fourteen (14) days prior to domestic departure and twenty-one (21) days prior to international travel.
- **Cabin Class Policy:**
  - **Economy / Coach:** Standard for all domestic flights and international flights under six (6) continuous flight hours.
  - **Premium Economy / Business:** Authorized exclusively for continuous international flights exceeding eight (8) flight hours, subject to prior VP approval.
- **Ground Transportation:** Employees are expected to utilize standard rideshare (UberX/Lyft Standard) or public transit. Car rentals are permitted only when multiple destinations make rideshare impractical; mid-size sedan or compact vehicles are the standard approved rental tier.

---

## 3. Lodging and Hotel Caps
- **Daily Rate Ceilings:** Nightly room rates must not exceed **$250 USD** (excluding local lodging taxes) for standard domestic metropolitan markets.
- **High-Cost Tier Cities:** For designated tier-one metropolitan areas (e.g., New York City, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo), the nightly ceiling is adjusted to **$350 USD**.
- **Room Extras:** Charges for personal hotel movies, minibar consumption, spa services, or laundry (on trips under 5 days) are non-reimbursable personal expenditures.

---

## 4. Meals and Per Diem Allowances
- **Daily Per Diem Cap:** Employees are eligible for a maximum total meal reimbursement of **$75.00 USD per day** while on overnight business travel.
- **Individual Meal Breakdown:** Recommended guidelines:
  - Breakfast: up to $15.00
  - Lunch: up to $20.00
  - Dinner: up to $40.00
- **Client Entertainment & Business Meals:** When hosting clients or prospects, expenses may exceed standard per diem caps provided the meal includes substantive business discourse. Prior manager approval and an explicit guest roster are required.
- **Alcohol Policy:** Moderate alcohol consumption with dinner or client entertainment is permissible (maximum 2 drinks per person), provided it is accompanied by food.

---

## 5. Receipt Documentation and Submission Deadlines
- **Mandatory Receipts:** Itemized receipts must be attached to the expense report for any individual transaction exceeding **$25.00 USD**. Credit card summary slips that do not detail purchased items will be rejected.
- **Submission Window:** Expense reports must be submitted through the finance management system within **thirty (30) calendar days** of the expense date. Claims submitted past sixty (60) days are non-reimbursable except under documented extenuating medical circumstances.
- **Reimbursement Processing:** Approved claims are deposited directly into the employee's designated payroll bank account within two (2) payroll cycles.

---

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following items are strictly non-reimbursable:
- Traffic, speeding, or parking violation fines.
- TSA PreCheck or Global Entry application fees (unless specifically designated for traveling executive staff).
- Personal grooming, toiletries, clothing purchases, or haircuts.
- First-class flight upgrades or airline club lounge day passes.
- Losses resulting from currency conversion fluctuations or airline reward points.
