# Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PTO-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 3.1  
**Applicability:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees of Apex Technologies, Inc.

---

## 1. Purpose and Overview
Apex Technologies recognizes the importance of personal well-being, rest, and work-life harmony. This policy establishes guidelines for accruing, requesting, and utilizing Paid Time Off (PTO) and other statutory and discretionary leave categories.

---

## 2. PTO Accrual and Tiers
Full-time employees accrue PTO on a semi-monthly basis across each calendar pay period according to their tenure with the company:

- **Tier 1 (0 to 2 Years of Service):** Accrue 15 days (120 hours) per calendar year, at a rate of 5.0 hours per pay period.
- **Tier 2 (2 to 5 Years of Service):** Accrue 20 days (160 hours) per calendar year, at a rate of 6.67 hours per pay period.
- **Tier 3 (5+ Years of Service):** Accrue 25 days (200 hours) per calendar year, at a rate of 8.33 hours per pay period.
- **Part-Time Employees:** Employees scheduled to work between 20 and 31 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis proportional to standard weekly hours.

---

## 3. PTO Request Procedure and Advance Notice
- **Notice Requirements:** For scheduled absences exceeding three (3) consecutive business days, employees must submit a formal PTO request through the HR portal at least two (2) weeks in advance.
- **Single-Day Absences:** Single-day PTO requests should be submitted at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance, subject to manager approval.
- **Peak Business Periods:** PTO requests during critical operational milestones or quarter-end financial closing may be subject to black-out periods as designated by department leads.

---

## 4. Rollover Caps and Use-It-or-Lose-It Provisions
- **Annual Rollover Cap:** Employees may carry over a maximum of forty (40) hours (5 business days) of unused accrued PTO into the subsequent calendar year.
- **Rollover Deadline:** Any carried-over hours exceeding the 40-hour limit that are not utilized by March 31 of the new calendar year are forfeited, subject to applicable state and local labor laws.
- **PTO Cash-Out:** Apex Technologies does not offer annual PTO cash-out options during active employment.

---

## 5. Sick Leave and Medical Absences
- **Dedicated Sick Time:** In addition to general PTO, all full-time employees receive eight (8) paid sick days (64 hours) per calendar year on January 1. Sick leave does not roll over year-to-year.
- **Physician Certification:** For continuous sick leave absences exceeding three (3) consecutive working days, an employee must furnish a signed medical certificate from a licensed healthcare provider prior to returning to duty.

---

## 6. Parental Leave
- **Primary Caregiver Leave:** Eligible employees who have completed at least six (6) continuous months of service are entitled to sixteen (16) weeks of 100% paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- **Secondary Caregiver Leave:** Secondary caregivers are eligible for eight (8) weeks of fully paid leave.
- **Timing:** Parental leave must be taken within twelve (12) months following the qualifying event and may be taken as a continuous block or in two split increments with manager approval.
- **Concurrent PTO Restriction:** Employees are strictly prohibited from stacking or using accrued general PTO hours concurrently with paid parental leave to receive double pay. However, employees may elect to take up to two (2) weeks (80 hours) of accrued PTO consecutively immediately following the conclusion of their parental leave.
- **PTO Accrual During Leave:** Employees on fully paid parental leave continue to accrue standard PTO hours during the approved leave period; unpaid personal leaves exceeding thirty (30) days suspend PTO accrual.

---

## 7. Bereavement Leave
- **Immediate Family:** Employees are granted up to five (5) consecutive paid days of bereavement leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or domestic partner).
- **Extended Family:** Up to three (3) paid days are provided for extended family members (grandparents, in-laws, legal guardians). Additional unpaid days or PTO may be requested for international travel.

---

## 8. Separation of Employment and Final Payout
Upon voluntary resignation or involuntary termination in good standing:
- Accrued, unused PTO is paid out on the final paycheck at the employee's regular hourly base rate, up to a maximum cap of eighty (80) hours.
- Unused sick leave and carried-over rollover hours are non-compensable upon separation.
