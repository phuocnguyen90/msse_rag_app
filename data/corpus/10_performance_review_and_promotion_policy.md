# Performance Review, Promotion, and Career Growth Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PER-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Applicability:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees of Apex Technologies, Inc.

---

## 1. Philosophy and Cycle Frequency
Apex Technologies believes continuous feedback drives professional excellence. The company conducts formal performance evaluations on a **bi-annual cadence**:
- **Mid-Year Check-In:** June 1 to June 30 (development-focused dialogue and objective alignment).
- **Year-End Performance Review:** December 1 to December 31 (evaluative review tied to compensation and promotion decisions).

---

## 2. Evaluation Criteria and 5-Point Rating Scale
Employees are evaluated across core competencies and goal achievements using a calibrated 5-point scale:
- **Rating 5 (Exceptional Impact):** Consistently surpasses targets; demonstrates industry-leading innovation; role model for cultural values.
- **Rating 4 (Exceeds Expectations):** Frequently outperforms expectations; delivers high-quality work with minimal guidance.
- **Rating 3 (Consistently Meets Expectations):** Solid, dependable contributor; achieves key quarterly OKRs and performance indicators.
- **Rating 2 (Needs Improvement):** Inconsistent deliverables; misses deadlines or fails to meet defined core role requirements.
- **Rating 1 (Unsatisfactory):** Consistently fails to meet acceptable performance standards; requires immediate intervention.

---

## 3. Merit Increases and Promotion Cadence
- **Merit Increase Effective Date:** Salary adjustments stemming from year-end reviews take effect on **March 1** of the following calendar year.
- **Promotion Windows:** Formal job title promotions and band adjustments are executed twice annually following each review cycle (effective March 1 and September 1).

---

## 4. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Guidelines
- **Initiation Trigger:** An employee receiving a Rating 1 or prolonged Rating 2 will be placed on a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- **Duration:** The standard PIP duration is **sixty (60) calendar days**.
- **Plan Structure:** A PIP clearly delineates specific deficient areas, concrete measurable objectives, mandatory milestones, and weekly progress check-ins with the manager and HR business partner.
- **Outcome:** Successful completion of all PIP milestones restores the employee to good standing. Failure to achieve the required performance standards by the 60th day leads to immediate termination of employment.
