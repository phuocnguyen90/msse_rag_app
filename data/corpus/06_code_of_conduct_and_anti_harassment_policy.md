# Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy

**Document ID:** POL-COC-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 3.3  
**Applicability:** All Board Members, Officers, Full-Time Employees, Contractors, and Interns of Apex Technologies, Inc.

---

## 1. Core Ethical Principles
Apex Technologies is committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and ethical workplace where every individual is treated with dignity. We maintain zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or dishonest business conduct.

---

## 2. Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination
- **Prohibited Conduct:** Harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or genetic information is strictly forbidden.
- **Sexual Harassment:** Includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, inappropriate physical contact, sexually explicit jokes or imagery, and quid-pro-quo employment arrangements.
- **Reporting Channels:** Any employee experiencing or witnessing harassment should report it immediately to their manager, Human Resources (`hr-ethics@apextech.internal`), or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline.

---

## 3. Conflicts of Interest and Outside Employment
- **Outside Business Activities:** Employees may not engage in secondary employment, consulting, or board advisory roles that compete directly with Apex Technologies or create an actual or perceived conflict of interest.
- **Moonlighting Disclosure:** Any external commercial activities must be formally disclosed to and approved in writing by Legal & Compliance prior to commencement.

---

## 4. Gifts, Entertainment, and Anti-Bribery
- **Gift Acceptance Ceiling and Boundary Condition:** Employees may accept business gifts or promotional items of nominal value only, not to exceed **$50.00 USD** in aggregate value per vendor per calendar year. Non-cash gifts valued at **exactly $50.00 USD or less** may be retained by the employee. Any gift valued at **$50.01 USD or greater** must be formally registered in the Corporate Gift Log within five (5) business days and surrendered to Human Resources for company-wide charitable distribution.
- **Vendor-Provided Hospitality & Meals:** Modest working lunches provided during commercial vendor meetings are permitted. However, vendor-hosted dinners, luxury entertainment, or event tickets exceeding **$100.00 USD per attendee** require advance written clearance from Legal & Compliance. (Note: For company-funded client entertainment hosted by Apex staff, refer to the $150.00 VP-approved allowance under POL-EXP-2025).
- **Cash Prohibition:** Accepting cash, gift cards, loans, or stock equivalents from any customer, supplier, or competitor is strictly illegal and grounds for immediate termination.
- **Government Officials:** Under the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA), providing any gift, meal, entertainment, or hospitality of any value to government officials is prohibited without prior written clearance from General Counsel.

---

## 5. Disciplinary Sanctions
Violations of this Code of Conduct will result in progressive disciplinary action up to and including:
1. Written formal reprimand in personnel file.
2. Mandatory retraining on ethics and compliance.
3. Suspension of employment without pay.
4. Immediate termination of employment for cause.
5. Referral to civil or criminal law enforcement agencies when applicable.
