# Holidays and Working Hours Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HOL-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Applicability:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees of Apex Technologies, Inc.

---

## 1. Standard Working Hours
- **Workweek Schedule:** The standard full-time workweek consists of forty (40) hours, typically scheduled Monday through Friday, eight (8) hours per day.
- **Core Hours:** Team members must be available and responsive during core collaboration hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST.
- **Meal and Rest Breaks:** Employees working shifts of six (6) hours or longer are entitled to an unpaid meal break of at least thirty (30) minutes and two paid fifteen (15) minute rest breaks.

---

## 2. Company-Observed Paid Holidays
Apex Technologies observes eleven (11) official paid company holidays each calendar year:
1. New Year's Day (January 1)
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Third Monday in January)
3. Presidents' Day (Third Monday in February)
4. Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
5. Juneteenth National Independence Day (June 19)
6. Independence Day (July 4)
7. Labor Day (First Monday in September)
8. Indigenous Peoples' Day / Columbus Day (Second Monday in October)
9. Veterans Day (November 11)
10. Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday in November)
11. Christmas Day (December 25)

*Holiday Weekend Rule:* If an observed holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. If a holiday falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

---

## 3. Floating Holidays
- **Annual Allocation:** In addition to observed national holidays, full-time employees are awarded two (2) paid Floating Holidays (16 hours) on January 1 of each calendar year.
- **Usage:** Floating holidays may be used for personal cultural observances, religious holy days, birthdays, or family celebrations.
- **Forfeiture:** Floating holidays must be taken within the calendar year granted and do not roll over or cash out upon separation.

---

## 4. Overtime and Compensatory Time (Non-Exempt Employees)
- **Eligibility:** Overtime compensation applies exclusively to employees classified as non-exempt under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA). Exempt salaried personnel are not eligible for overtime pay.
- **Overtime Rate:** Non-exempt employees will be paid one and one-half (1.5x) times their regular base hourly rate for all approved hours worked beyond forty (40) hours in a single workweek.
- **Prior Authorization:** All overtime must be pre-approved in writing by the employee's direct manager prior to working the extra hours.

---

## 5. Engineering On-Call and Incident Standby
- **On-Call Rotation:** Site Reliability Engineers (SRE) and core infrastructure engineers participating in formal primary on-call rotations receive a flat standby stipend of **$350.00 USD per 7-day shift**.
- **Incident Response Pay:** When actively paged outside regular working hours to remediate Tier-1/Tier-2 service outages, non-exempt on-call engineers are compensated at their overtime rate for a minimum of two (2) hours per incident page.
