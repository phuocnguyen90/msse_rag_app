# Employee Benefits, Health, and Wellness Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BEN-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Applicability:** All Regular Full-Time Employees of Apex Technologies, Inc.

---

## 1. Comprehensive Healthcare Benefits
- **Coverage Effective Date:** Health, dental, and vision insurance coverage takes effect on the **first day of the calendar month following the date of hire**.
- **Plan Options:** Apex Technologies offers three comprehensive tiers through national carriers:
  1. High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with Health Savings Account (HSA) contribution.
  2. Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) Plan.
  3. Health Maintenance Organization (HMO) Plan.
- **Premium Subsidies:** Apex covers 90% of individual employee premiums and 75% of eligible dependent and family premiums.

---

## 2. 401(k) Retirement Savings and Employer Matching
- **Eligibility:** Full-time and part-time employees are eligible to enroll immediately upon their date of hire.
- **Matching Contribution:** Apex Technologies matches **100% of employee contributions up to the first five percent (5%)** of the employee's eligible annual base salary.
- **Vesting Schedule:** Employer matching contributions are **100% immediately vested** upon deposit with zero cliff or graded vesting delays.

---

## 3. Wellness Stipend and Fitness Allowance
- **Monthly Fitness Subsidy:** Regular full-time employees are eligible for a monthly wellness stipend of **$60.00 USD** to cover gym memberships, studio classes, fitness trackers, or meditation app subscriptions.
- **Mental Health Days:** In addition to regular PTO and sick time, Apex provides two (2) designated company-wide "Recharge & Wellness Days" per year, scheduled in May and October.

---

## 4. Professional Development and Continuing Education
- **Annual Education Budget:** Full-time staff who have completed at least six (6) months of tenure are eligible for up to **$1,500.00 USD per calendar year** in reimbursed tuition, technical certifications, industry conference admissions, and educational textbooks.
- **Pre-Approval:** Coursework and conferences must be approved by the employee's direct manager prior to financial commitment.
