# Company Equipment and Bring-Your-Own-Device (BYOD) Policy

**Document ID:** POL-EQP-2025  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Applicability:** All Personnel utilizing Apex Technologies hardware or personal devices for business communications.

---

## 1. Corporate Hardware Provisioning
- **Primary Workstation:** Full-time technical staff receive a standard corporate laptop (Apple MacBook Pro 16" M-series or Dell XPS 15 Developer Edition) pre-configured with corporate endpoint protection and full-disk encryption (FileVault / BitLocker).
- **Hardware Refresh Lifecycle:** Corporate laptops are scheduled for standard replacement every **thirty-six (36) months** of continuous service.
- **Ownership & Custody:** All supplied computers, monitors, and accessories remain the exclusive physical and intellectual property of Apex Technologies.

---

## 2. Bring-Your-Own-Device (BYOD) Guidelines
- **Eligibility:** Employees desiring to access corporate email, Slack, or calendar on personal smartphones or tablets may do so under the BYOD policy.
- **Mobile Device Management (MDM):** Any personal smartphone connecting to corporate email or internal services must enroll in the corporate MDM solution (Microsoft Intune / Jamf).
- **Remote Wipe Authorization:** By enrolling in BYOD, the employee acknowledges that Apex IT retains the capability and legal right to remotely wipe corporate containers, profiles, and associated data upon device loss or employee termination.
- **Mobile Phone Stipend:** Full-time employees whose job roles require active mobile availability (engineering on-call, sales, executive leadership) receive a monthly cellular stipend of **$50.00 USD** through payroll.

---

## 3. Loss, Theft, and Damage Protocol
- **Reporting Window:** In the event of loss or theft of any corporate laptop, phone, or MFA security key, the employee must notify the IT Helpdesk and Security Operations (`security-incident@apextech.internal`) within **twenty-four (24) hours** of the occurrence.
- **Police Report:** Stolen corporate equipment requires a verified police report number submitted alongside the IT incident ticket.
- **Equipment Return upon Separation:** All issued laptops, power adapters, security keys, and accessories must be returned to the IT department within five (5) business days following the last day of active employment.
