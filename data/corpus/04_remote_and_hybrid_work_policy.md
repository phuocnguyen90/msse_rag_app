# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RMT-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Applicability:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees of Apex Technologies, Inc.

---

## 1. Philosophy and Objectives
Apex Technologies embraces a flexible work culture that combines the benefits of in-person collaboration with the productivity of remote work. This policy establishes operating guidelines, communication expectations, and equipment support for remote and hybrid personnel.

---

## 2. Work Arrangement Categories
Employees are assigned one of three operational designations in their formal offer letter:

1. **Office-Based:** Employees whose core duties require physical presence on-site 4 to 5 days per week (e.g., facilities management, hardware lab engineers).
2. **Hybrid:** Employees expected to work at least two (2) designated anchor days per week in an Apex office, with the remaining days worked remotely. Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated universal company anchor days.
3. **Fully Remote:** Employees residing greater than fifty (50) miles from an Apex Technologies office facility, with no recurring in-office requirement.

---

## 3. Core Working Hours and Availability
- **Core Hours:** All employees, regardless of location, must be online, reachable, and active on corporate communication channels (Slack, email, Google Meet) during core collaboration hours: **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST)**, Monday through Friday.
- **Calendar Visibility:** Working hours and time zones must be accurately maintained on Google Calendar. Status indicators on Slack must accurately reflect when an employee is in a meeting, out to lunch, or away from their desk.
- **Meeting Etiquette:** Hybrid and remote team members are expected to have web cameras enabled for team meetings, customer presentations, and company all-hands, unless an audio-only exception is granted by the organizer.

---

## 4. Home Office Equipment Stipend
- **Initial Setup Stipend:** Newly hired hybrid and fully remote employees receive a one-time reimbursement stipend of up to **$500.00 USD** to procure home office equipment (ergonomic chair, external monitor, keyboard, mouse, or desk riser).
- **Claim Process:** Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within ninety (90) days of the employee's start date.
- **Ownership:** Peripherals and ergonomic furnishings purchased with the stipend remain the personal property of the employee upon separation.

---

## 5. Internet and Connectivity Standards
- **High-Speed Requirement:** Remote workstations must maintain a stable broadband connection with a minimum download speed of **50 Mbps** and upload speed of **10 Mbps**.
- **Monthly Internet Subsidy:** Apex Technologies provides a recurring monthly internet reimbursement of **$40.00 USD** for eligible remote and hybrid personnel, processed automatically through payroll.
- **Backup Plan:** In the event of home internet outages exceeding two (2) hours, employees are expected to transition to a nearby co-working space, mobile cellular hotspot, or corporate office facility.

---

## 6. Remote Information Security
- **Physical Security:** Remote employees must ensure family members, housemates, and visitors cannot observe confidential client information or internal communications displayed on computer screens.
- **Secure Networking:** Working from shared co-working spaces or public locations requires mandatory use of corporate VPN and privacy screen filters on laptops.
- **Work Area Safety:** Employees must maintain a hazard-free home workspace. Workers' compensation coverage applies solely to work-related injuries sustained within the designated home office area during scheduled work hours.
